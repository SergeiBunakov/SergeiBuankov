About me

Hi! I am Sergei Bunakov, a 3D Hard Surface Artist and Experimental Animation student at OCAD University. My focus lies in creating optimized assets, props, architecture, and environments for games and game engines, with strong capabilities in texture/PBR material creation and UV unwrapping.

I got into 3D for two reasons: a passion for urbanism, architecture, and cities, and a technical fascination with indie games. I love digging into the files and assets of them to see how they're built. Because of this, my skillset is quite scattered, and I work with a wide range of software.
