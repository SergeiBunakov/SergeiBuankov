Description

This architectural model explores the styles of the Mediterranean basin and colonial architecture, combining elements from Southern European countries such as Italy, Montenegro, and Croatia. I carefully crafted classic elements to capture the region's authentic character, featuring warm, textured stucco walls reminiscent of centuries old construction, designed to reflect the bright southern sun. The structure is crowned by a classic, sloping roof clad in terracotta tiles, which adds a splash of earthy color. Balconies with stone balustrades offer a balance of ornamentation and function, while the base of the building is defined by arched forms, including the entrance and large windows. This project was a deliberate departure from my usual focus on contemporary architecture, with the goal of delving into a more classical, heritage-driven design. Through it, I aimed to experiment with natural materials while maintaining a balance between aesthetic beauty and practical functionality.
