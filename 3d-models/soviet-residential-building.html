Maya 3D Substance Painter Photoshop

Description

This is one of my first architectural models. As someone originally from Russia, I spent my childhood in a Khrushchyovka, surrounded by similar concrete buildings from various eras.

While that environment of efficient, boxy forms was my initial inspiration, my goal was not to replicate it, but to reimagine it. Moscow, as the USSR's primary city, was also a hub for experimental, one-of-a-kind architecture rarely seen elsewhere. Drawing on that spirit, I introduced curved balconies that deliberately break from the characteristic blocky aesthetics, envisioning a more fluid alternative to the architecture I knew.

The model also features light in the windows, achieved using emission texture maps. I think everyone from my generation in post-Soviet countries is familiar with the aesthetic of a night city: endless rows of glowing buildings filling the streets with warm, orange spots. This picture, familiar from countless films, evokes a strange nostalgia for a time I never experienced. So, I've recreated not just architecture, but that very feeling - the warm and melancholic sensation of a Soviet night city, familiar to me since childhood.
